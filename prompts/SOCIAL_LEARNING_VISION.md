# PIXELSPACE AI CLASSROOM — SOCIAL LEARNING PLATFORM VISION

## Positioning

**Learn with AI. Create together. Grow with your community.**

Pixelspace AI Classroom should be positioned as an **AI-powered social learning platform** rather than another AI Learning Management System (LMS).

The distinction matters: do not build a social network with AI features added on. Build a learning platform where AI helps students create knowledge, and the community makes that knowledge discoverable, collaborative, and engaging.

That could become Pixelspace's biggest differentiator.

---

## 1. The proposed vision

Combine the best aspects of these products:

- **NotebookLM — The learning engine.** Students create workbooks, upload learning sources, ask AI questions, and generate quizzes, slides, notes and flashcards.
- **Pinterest — The discovery engine.** Students explore beautifully presented workbooks, save learning resources, and build collections around subjects they love.
- **GitHub — The creation engine.** Students remix approved workbooks, improve shared resources, and give attribution to original creators.
- **Duolingo — The motivation engine.** Learning streaks, personal milestones, badges, club challenges and meaningful achievements make students want to return.

## 2. How to restructure the application

Instead of treating Community as one extra menu, make social learning a core part of the product.

### Proposed student navigation

- **Utama** — Personal home
- **Buku Kerja** — AI workspace
- **Terokai** — Discover
- **Komuniti** — People & clubs
- **Hasil Karya** — My creations
- **Tugasan** — Schoolwork

The distinction between **Terokai** and **Komuniti** matters. Terokai is where students find great learning content. Komuniti is where they connect through shared academic interests.

### The core learning loop

A continuous cycle of discovery, AI-assisted learning, creation and sharing.

Realistic example:

A Year 5 student discovers a workbook called *Sistem Suria* created by another student. They open it, ask the AI tutor to explain planetary orbits, complete a quiz, and generate a new slide presentation.

They then create their own version, adding illustrations and explanations. Once approved, they publish it for other students to explore.

That is much more interesting than simply uploading homework to an LMS.

## 3. The feature that could make this special: student creators

Introduce **Pixelspace Creator Profiles**.

Every student has a learning identity — not a conventional social-media profile.

Example concept:

> **BintangSains**
> Pencipta Pembelajaran · Tahun 5
> Sains · STEM
> 12 Buku Kerja · 4 Koleksi · 8 Lencana
>
> Buku Kerja Pilihan:
> - Sistem Suria — Sains · Tahun 5
> - Eksperimen Sains — Sains · Tahun 5

Illustrative profile concept. Public aliases, publications and visibility would be controlled by school policies.

This creates a meaningful motivation: students can become known for being good at explaining mathematics, creating beautiful science slides, or helping others understand a topic.

But because the platform serves children, do not include public direct messaging, unrestricted follower discovery, public popularity rankings, or location-based discovery. Students should be able to admire someone's work without being able to track their private activities or contact them freely.

## 4. Primary and secondary students should have different social experiences

| | Sekolah Rendah | Sekolah Menengah |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Avatar + safe alias | Academic creator profile |
| Discovery | Curated workbooks | More advanced search and discovery |
| Community | Teacher-managed clubs | Subject and exam-focused clubs |
| Sharing | Approval-based | Approval-based with more creation options |
| Collaboration | Guided activities | Workbook remixing and projects |
| Motivation | Badges and missions | Portfolios and academic milestones |
| Visual style | Playful 3D illustrations | Cleaner, more mature UI |

One codebase, one platform, two age-appropriate experiences.

## 5. The business opportunity is bigger than an LMS

Two different types of value:

- **For students:** A place where learning is social, creative and personally rewarding.
- **For schools:** A controlled AI learning environment with curriculum alignment, teacher oversight, assignments, analytics and governance.

This means the product can be sold to schools without making the student experience feel like school administration software.

For the four-school pilot, start with **school-private communities** and a small curated collection of cross-school workbooks. There are not yet enough real students and content to rely on a nationwide social feed. Seed it with approved teacher-created material, then measure whether students actually discover, save, remix and complete workbooks.

## 6. What to build first

Prioritize in the following order:

1. **AI Workbook** — The product's core utility must be genuinely useful.
2. **Discover** — Beautiful, searchable, school-approved workbooks.
3. **Creator Profiles** — Give students a reason to create quality learning materials.
4. **Save, Share & Remix** — The most important social learning interactions.
5. **Clubs & Challenges** — Build recurring participation.
6. **Personalized Feed** — Add it once there is enough real community activity.

Do not build a full Instagram-like feed first. Without enough student activity, it will feel empty, and it introduces moderation complexity before the core value is proven.

## Final recommendation

Position Pixelspace AI Classroom as an **AI-powered social learning platform for Malaysian schools**.

Keep the NotebookLM-inspired AI workbook as the foundation, make discovery and remixing the social layer, and keep assignments, teacher tools, curriculum mapping and school governance in the background supporting the experience.

The long-term opportunity isn't just students using AI to finish schoolwork. It's students building a **shared library of knowledge for other students**, with AI helping them learn, explain and create.
